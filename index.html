CS109 concepts applied
Generating 1,000 future Earths
Independent samples from 8 variable distributions
xi(k) ~ Fi(μi, σi),  k = 1…1000
Monte Carlo
Fitting distribution parameters
From real datasets: methane satellite data, Drawdown impact ranges, IPCC AR6 intervals
μ̂ = (1/n)Σxi  ·  σ̂² = (1/n)Σ(xi−μ̂)²
MLE
Eliminating inconsistent futures
Selecting a variable reveals its value; particles outside ±1.2σ are removed
Sj = { x : |xj − μj| ≤ 1.2σj }
Bayesian
Measuring remaining uncertainty
Shannon entropy over 10 warming bins from 1.5°C to 4.0°C
H = −Σ pi log2(pi)
Entropy
Ranking what matters most
Information value = impact weight × uncertainty. Tipping points rank 15× above solar.
ΔH = Hbefore − Hafter  ·  I = 2·w·σ
